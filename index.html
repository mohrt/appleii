First, choose your Apple and load its ROMs

The Apple can't switch on without the ROM chips Apple built into it. They are still under copyright, so they aren't included here: choose or drop your own ROM image files. They stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere. The browser remembers them, so you only need to do this once for each model.

  • Apple IIe ROM Required

    Applesoft BASIC, the Monitor and the built-in firmware: 16 KB (16,384 bytes), or the two 8 KB chips it comes as. The enhanced IIe's (342-0304 and 342-0303) runs the most software; the original IIe's (342-0135 and 342-0134) works too.

    Often named Apple2e_Enhanced.rom, Apple2e.rom, or 342-0304-a.e10 and 342-0303-a.e8

  • Apple IIc ROM Required

    The IIc's firmware: 16 KB (16,384 bytes) for the first IIc, or 32 KB (32,768 bytes) for ROM 0, 3 or 4 (342-0033, 342-0445). The IIc boots its drive from this ROM, so it needs no Disk II ROM.

    Often named apple2c.rom or 342-0445-b.bin

  • Video ROM Required

    The characters on the screen, chip 342-0265 (the IIc's 341-0265 is the same; the original IIe's is 342-0133). 4 KB (4,096 bytes).

    Often named Apple2e_Enhanced_Video.rom or 342-0265-a.chr

  • Apple II Plus ROM Required

    Applesoft BASIC and the Autostart Monitor. 12 KB (12,288 bytes).

    Often named Apple2_Plus.rom or apple2p.rom

  • Apple II ROM Required

    Integer BASIC and the original Monitor. 12 KB (12,288 bytes). The Apple II starts in the Monitor: type C600G and Return to boot a disk.

    Often named Apple2.rom

  • Character ROM Required

    The Apple II's and II Plus's 64 characters, chip 341-0036. 2 KB (2,048 bytes).

    Often named Apple2_Video.rom

  • Disk II ROM Required

    The disk controller's boot ROM, chip 341-0027 (16 sectors). 256 bytes.

    Often named DISK2.rom or 341-0027-a.p5

Pick several files at once, or a .zip containing them (a whole ROM collection works too). The AppleWin emulator keeps them in its resource folder.

Release to load these ROMs

Drive 1 No disk has saved games
Drive 2 No disk has saved games

Drop .dsk, .do, .po, .nib, .woz or .zip files anywhere on the page to put them in drive 1 (onto drive 2 for drive 2). Several at once, such as all the disks of a game, give each drive a Swap menu for changing disks.

Release to insert

Joystick
Click or press a key to start sound

Left Alt (Option) is Open Apple and Right Alt Closed Apple; Ctrl+F12 is Ctrl-Reset, and with Open Apple held it restarts. To play a joystick game on the keyboard, choose keys for the joystick (keys it uses don't type on the Apple, so set it back to No keys for typing). Gamepads work without taking any keys, the stick as an analogue joystick.
